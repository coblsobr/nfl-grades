How the grades work

Every grade is built only from free, public football data. Nothing is copied from any paid grading service.

  • Ranks first: behind every rank is a grade built from the stats below; the app shows the rank because that's what means something.
  • What goes in: the stats that best separate good players from bad at each position, like points added per play for quarterbacks, yards per route for receivers, pressure rate for pass rushers and passer rating allowed for corners. Tap any player to see exactly which pieces make up his grade.
  • Who gets ranked: everyone who played gets a grade, but only players with at least about half a starter's workload get a rank. A backup with a few snaps is listed separately and his grade is pulled toward a typical backup's.
  • Each season stands alone. "With playoffs" adds a finished season's postseason games.
  • Coaching: aggressiveness compares 4th-down decisions with what the rest of the league does in the same spot and counts timid punts; play calling looks at early-down efficiency, play-action payoff and predictability; "beat expectations" compares wins with the Vegas lines. None of it can tell who actually called a play.
  • Teams: offense, defense and special teams are measured in points added per play, adjusted for the strength of the opponents faced.
  • Limits: offensive line grades are estimates, because free data doesn't say which lineman allowed a pressure. Tight end blocking isn't graded yet.

Data: nflverse play-by-play, FTN Data via nflverse (CC-BY-SA 4.0), NFL Next Gen Stats and Pro Football Reference.
